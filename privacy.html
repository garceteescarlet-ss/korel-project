Your Data

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

What We Collect

We collect what you choose to give us. If you submit the consulting inquiry form or the checkout form, we receive your name and email address, plus anything else you type into those fields. We don’t collect anything beyond what’s in the form itself.

The Age Gate

When you confirm you’re 21 or older, that choice is saved using your browser’s local storage — a small piece of data kept on your own device, so you’re not asked again on your next visit. It never leaves your browser and isn’t sent to us.

How We Use It

Anything you submit through the consulting form is used only to respond to your inquiry. Anything you submit at checkout is used only to fulfill and confirm your order. We don’t sell or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes.

Payment Processing

KOREL doesn’t collect or store your card details. When you check out, payment is handled by a third-party processor (such as Stripe, Payhip, or Gumroad), who has their own privacy policy governing how they handle your payment information.

Cookies & Tracking

This site doesn’t use tracking cookies or analytics. The only thing stored in your browser is the age-gate preference described above.

Your Rights

If you’d like us to delete any information you’ve submitted to us, or have questions about this policy, reach out through our consulting page and we’ll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date below.
